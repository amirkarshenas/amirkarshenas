# 👋 Hi, I’m Amir Karshenas

### 💡 Software Architect | .NET Advocate | Research Technologist

I’m a Senior Software Architect at [RTI International](https://www.rti.org), where I design and modernize large-scale **.NET applications** supporting scientific and public-health research for federal agencies such as the **CDC, BJS, and EPA**.  

With over 20 years of experience in **C#, ASP.NET Core, EF Core, Azure Functions, and DevOps**, I focus on bridging **modern software architecture and scientific discovery**. My passion lies in applying .NET to research domains where it hasn’t traditionally been used — helping transform data into insight at national scale.

---

### 🧩 What I Work On
- Architecting secure, cloud-native research systems using **.NET 8, Azure, and SQL Server**
- Building **APIs and integration pipelines** that connect field data to analytical workflows
- Leading modernization of **federal health and environmental reporting platforms**
- Mentoring teams in adopting modern .NET frameworks, DevOps, and clean architecture

---

### 🌍 My Mission
> “To expand the use of .NET in the scientific and nonprofit world — making data-driven research more accessible, secure, and reproducible.”
