### 💡 About Me

I’m a **Senior Software Architect** at [RTI International](https://www.rti.org), a scientific nonprofit research institute. For more than two decades, I’ve worked at the intersection of **scientific research and software engineering**, designing scalable, secure, and data-driven systems for the **CDC**, **BJS**, **EPA**, and other federal agencies.

My work spans **AI, data mining, information retrieval, and software architecture**—connecting research questions with practical tools and reliable systems. **C# and .NET remain a strong foundation** in my work, alongside Python, SQL, JavaScript, and the wider AI and data ecosystem.

I believe good engineering starts with understanding the problem. I choose languages, frameworks, and platforms to fit the research, the people using the software, and the needs of the system over time.
