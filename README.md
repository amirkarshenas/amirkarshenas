### 💡 About Me

I’m a **Senior Software Architect** at [RTI International](https://www.rti.org), a leading scientific nonprofit research institute.  
For more than two decades, I’ve used **.NET** to bridge the gap between **scientific discovery** and **modern software engineering** — designing scalable, secure, and data-driven systems for the **CDC**, **BJS**, **EPA**, and other federal agencies.

My focus is on making .NET the language of science — powering reproducible research, automation, and large-scale analytics across disciplines.

### 🧠 What I Do

✅ **Architect Enterprise-Grade .NET Systems**  
Design large distributed platforms using ASP.NET Core, EF Core, Azure Functions, and RESTful APIs.

✅ **Advance .NET in Scientific Research**  
Integrate modern .NET architecture into research workflows, improving transparency and reproducibility of national health and environmental studies.

✅ **Mentor and Lead Teams**  
Guide software engineers and scientists in adopting modern .NET, DevOps, CI/CD, and clean architecture patterns.

✅ **Promote Open & Reproducible Science**  
Encourage open-source collaboration and the use of .NET in academic and nonprofit research.
